# Niiikotopia: Human — Narrative & Game Design Bible

## 1. Plot Summaries

### Short Description (Storefront & Elevator Pitch)

After twenty years in a brutal deep-space penal colony, escaped convict Ethan crash-lands on an uncharted sanctuary inhabited by fragile, cat-eared demihumans called Niiikos. Hired by his own greed to sell them on the black market, Ethan must choose between buying back his legal humanity or dying in the mud to shield an innocent species when automated corporate harvesters darken the sky.

  

### Normal Summary (Production Overview)

Ethan, an aging criminal haunted by a sky-ship disaster that killed innocent children twenty years ago, escapes a colonial prison station in a stolen rogue shuttle. Running out of hyper-jump fuel, he crashes onto a quiet planet populated by 1.2-meter tall demihumans known as Niiikos (Hucats)—a species prized on the galactic black market for their ears and organs. Wearing a mask of cold cynicism, Ethan plans to use the brilliant mechanic Tania to repair his ship so he can contact his syndicate boss and auction the entire village off in exchange for a clean colonial identity card.

  

As days pass under the gentle care of Nya the researcher, Tania's earnest admiration, and the feral suspicion of Lily the scavenger, Ethan's twenty years of prison conditioning begin to crack. When an automated fleet from the Human Colony Defense Corporation descends from orbit to harvest the Niiiko population with non-lethal stun drones, Ethan's selfish plan collapses into a desperate war for survival. Armed with Tania’s volatile gunpowder prototypes, Ethan fights a grueling battle to buy time for a mass evacuation ark. In the final minutes, faced with an automated anti-air battery locking onto the fleeing ark and Tania offering to blow herself up so he can escape, Ethan throws Tania and Lily into his own shuttle, locks the hatch from the outside, and stays behind in the fire—trading his legal freedom to die as a real man.

  

### Long Description (Complete Narrative Breakdown)

For two decades, Ethan was not legally a person; he was Inmate #7412 on an orbital penal station, stripped of human rights after a reckless flying transport accident left dozens of colonial children dead in burning wreckage. Broken by guilt, he had surrendered himself, enduring twenty years of shock batons, isolation tanks, and subhuman treatment while obsessing over a single dream: escaping to buy a forged citizen ID and becoming legally "human" again. Seizing a rare prison riot to steal a small rogue shuttle, Ethan executes blind hyper-jumps until his fuel lines rupture, forcing a violent crash-landing into the forests of an unmapped planet.

  

Stepping out of the smoking wreck with an empty pistol, Ethan expects savage monsters. Instead, he is surrounded by Niiikos—delicate, 1.2-meter tall cat-eared demihumans wearing simple medical-style tunics and numbered plastic name tags. When Ethan aggressively grabs one, the villagers pelt him and his ruined hull with harmless, concentrated energy balls. Before violence escalates, a Niiiko wearing an oversized human lab coat steps forward holding a charcoal-drawn signboard, inviting the giant stranger to sleep in her hut. Over the next several days, this caretaker—Nya—teaches him to communicate through drawings. Ethan realizes from their name tags that this planet is an abandoned biological breeding sanctuary. His criminal instincts kick in: if he repairs his shuttle's transmitter, he can call his old syndicate, the Mad Dog Gang, and sell the village for millions, buying his permanent freedom.

  

To fix his ship, Ethan recruits Tania, a frantic Niiiko inventor with cybernetic gear fused to her head, while enduring the constant hostility of Lily, a feral collector who smells the prison rot on him and repeatedly tries to poison or stab him. While waiting on engine repairs, Ethan handles heavy labor for the village, carelessly patting the ears of every Niiiko who helps him—except Lily, who bristles whenever he gets close, secretly aching for the warmth she refuses to ask for. Along the way, Nya and Tania constantly ask him a question he can only answer with bitter cynicism: _"What is a human?"_

  

When the rogue shuttle is nearly operational, Ethan is presented with his first moral crossroads: take off alone and call the slavers, or stay a little longer. Before he can act, the sky turns black. Automated harvesting ships drop from the clouds, firing concentrated stun orbs that knock the villagers unconscious for industrial collection. Ethan initially tries to grab Tania and Lily to flee in his small ship, realizing he can at least sell or save the two of them, but Nya refuses to board, staying in the burning village to shield the unconscious children. Something inside Ethan snaps. Refusing to watch small creatures burn in wreckage a second time, he grabs Tania’s crude pneumatic launcher and dives into the trenches.

  

As the siege drags on, Tania discovers how to synthesize volatile black gunpowder, forging a heavy, scrap-iron kinetic rifle with recoil so violent only Ethan's large human frame can endure it. Pushing through bruised ribs and bleeding shoulders, Ethan holds the perimeter across multiple days while Tania constructs a massive makeshift ark to evacuate all 10,000 Niiikos. Just as Nya pilots the ark toward the upper atmosphere, a massive automated Anti-Air (AA) battery locks onto its hull. Ethan stands at a final crossroads: his rogue ship has just enough fuel for one jump. Tania, after hearing Ethan confess that he originally planned to sell them all, forgives him and offers to manually detonate the AA battery herself, with Lily stepping up to die beside her sister.

  

Looking at the empty pilot seat—his ticket to legal humanity—and looking at the two tiny girls preparing to die for him, Ethan makes his choice. He violently grabs Lily by the scruff of her tunic, hurls both her and Tania into the shuttle airlock, slams the manual lock from the outside, and triggers the automated deep-space launch. Listening to Lily beat her fists against the glass in tearful rage, Ethan turns his back on his freedom, charges into the teeth of the drones with his kinetic rifle, and shatters the AA battery's targeting core as the ark clears orbit. Pinned to the scorched mud by electrified harvester cables as his own shuttle streaks into hyperspace carrying Tania and Lily into an unknown dimension, Ethan drops his prison tag into the dirt—dying in chains, yet finally, completely human.

## 3. Character Profiles & Progressive Bios

### Ethan (Inmate #7412)

- **Role:** Protagonist / Escaped Colonial Convict
    
      
    
- **Height:** 1.88m
    
      
    
- **Favorite Activity:** Sitting in dead silence cleaning weapon bolts; carelessly ruffling Niiiko ears when he thinks nobody is watching.
    
      
    
- **Favorite Food:** Fire-roasted meat with real salt (after 20 years of gray prison nutrient paste, though Nya's warm vegetable broth becomes his secret comfort).
    
      
    

#### Unlockable Character Bios

- **Bio 1 (Unlocked at Act 1):** _An aging, heavily scarred convict who escaped the Tartarus-9 orbital penal station. Categorized as a subhuman asset by colonial law, Ethan views everyone as a tool or a threat. His only goal is to repair his stolen shuttle, trade high-value cargo to the underworld, and buy a clean citizen ID._
    
      
    
- **Bio 2 (Unlocked at Act 3):** _Before his twenty years in chains, Ethan was a reckless smuggler for the Mad Dog Gang. His career ended when his overloaded cargo transport lost thrusters over a civilian hab-block, killing dozens of children in the fire. Nobody caught him—he sat on the burning tarmac and turned himself in because he couldn't stand the sound of the sirens._
    
      
    
- **Bio 3 (Unlocked at Act 6):** _Despite his gruff bark and his confession that he planned to sell the village into slavery, Ethan has become the village's heavy shield. His collarbones are fractured from the kickback of Tania's gunpowder rifle, yet he refuses to step back from the trench line._
    
      
    
- **Bio 4 (Unlocked at Epilogue):** _He never got his colonial passport, his apartment, or his legal pardon. By throwing Tania and Lily into the airlock and dying in chains beneath the ruined AA battery, Inmate #7412 killed the monster inside himself and died a real man._
    
      
    

### Nya (Formerly Tonicat, The Enchanter / The Caretaker)

- **Role:** Village Leader & Biological Researcher
    
      
    
- **Height:** 1.20m
    
      
    
- **Base Attributes:** Cuteness: 100/100 | Physical Power: 2/100 | Magic Power: 2/100
    
      
    
- **Signature Quote:** _"Hmmm, what should I eat today?"_
    
      
    
      
    
- **Favorite Activity:** Sketching instructional charcoal drawings on wooden boards; organizing salvaged human books in the ruined lab.
    
      
    
- **Favorite Food:** Sweet forest berry stew and steamed root buns.
    
      
    

#### Unlockable Character Bios

- **Bio 1 (Unlocked at Act 1):** A gentle, soft-spoken Niiiko who wears a scavenged, oversized human laboratory coat. Despite having almost zero physical strength (2/100), she acts as the village's maternal caretaker, greeting Ethan with warm food and hand-drawn signboards instead of fear.
    
      
    
- **Bio 2 (Unlocked at Act 3):** _Nya is the only Niiiko on the planet who studied the faded human primers left behind in the sanctuary's central facility. She understands that the plastic name tags worn by her people were left by the "Tall Ones," and she views Ethan with deep empathy, recognizing the posture of a wounded creature._
    
      
    
- **Bio 3 (Unlocked at Act 7):** _When the sky harvesters descended, Nya's fragile body hid an iron spine. Refusing to abandon a single unconscious villager, she piloted the makeshift Colony Ark through orbital fire to lead 10,000 of her people into deep space._
    
      
    

### Tania, The Inventor

- **Role:** Chief Mechanic & Transmutation Prodigy
    
      
    
- **Height:** 1.18m
    
      
    
- **Base Attributes:** Cuteness: 90/100 | Physical Power: 1/100 | Magic Power: 5/100 (Class: Transmutation)
    
      
    
- **Signature Quote:** _"Waaaa Where did I put my X element"_
    
      
    
      
    
- **Favorite Activity:** Crawling inside cramped engine manifolds; taking apart human electronics just to see how the capacitors spark.
    
      
    
- **Favorite Food:** Crunchy roasted nuts and sugary energy rations she keeps forgetting in her coat pockets.
    
      
    

#### Unlockable Character Bios

- **Bio 1 (Unlocked at Act 2):** A scatterbrained mad scientist with cybernetic antenna gear grafted onto her head. Though physically the frailest Niiiko in the village (1/100 physical power), her transmutation intellect allows her to interface directly with salvaged human machinery.
    
      
    
- **Bio 2 (Unlocked at Act 5):** _Tania views Ethan with pure, uncritical adoration. To her, he isn't a scary criminal—he is a giant, fascinating partner who brings her real aerospace engines to fix. She happily accepts his heavy headpats while covered in motor grease._
    
      
    
- **Bio 3 (Unlocked at Act 6):** _Pushed to the brink by the invasion, Tania reverse-engineered ancient human chemical records to synthesize black gunpowder—forging the heavy kinetic rifle that allowed Ethan to shatter corporate drone armor._
    
      
    
- **Bio 4 (Unlocked at Epilogue):** _Even after Ethan confessed his dark intentions, Tania offered to blow herself up in the AA cannon's vents so he could fly home. Forced into Ethan's shuttle against her will, her optic sensors calculated his 0.0% survival rate as she was rocketed into an unknown dimension alongside Lily._

#### Unlockable Character Bios

- **Bio 1 (Unlocked at Act 2):** A wild, sharp-eyed treasure hunter with the highest physical agility of the trio (10/100 physical power). She smells the prison blood and greed on Ethan from day one and desperately wants to slit his throat.
    
      
    
- **Bio 2 (Unlocked at Act 4):** _Though she lacks technical genius, Lily is the village's immune system. She scavenges the raw scrap Tania needs to build inventions and watches Ethan like a hawk. Deep down, she harbors a childish, bitter resentment that Ethan pats every Niiiko's head except hers._
    
      
    
- **Bio 3 (Unlocked at Act 7):** _Fiercely loyal to Tania, Lily prepared to walk into the AA battery's fire just so her sister wouldn't die alone. When Ethan grabbed her by the scruff like a stray cat and threw her into the escaping shuttle, he broke her hatred forever—leaving her with an unpayable debt and a vow to drag him out of hell just to force his hand onto her ears._
    
      
    

## 4. World Lore, Factions & Codex Entries

### Bio-Corp & The Human Colony Defense Corporation (HCDC)

- **Category:** Antagonist Faction
    
      
    
- **Lore Details:** A sprawling interstellar conglomerate that masks its commercial harvesting operations behind the title of "Colony Defense." Across the outer rim, Hucats are classified not as sentient citizens, but as Tier-1 Biological Commodities. Because Hucat ears and internal organs fetch astronomical prices among wealthy colonial elites for rejuvenation medicine and luxury delicacies, Bio-Corp secretly seeds remote, habitable planets as free-range breeding sanctuaries. Every few decades, when the population reaches 10,000+, an automated harvesting fleet is dispatched to collect the crop.
    
      
    

### The Automated Harvesting Fleet & AA Grid

- **Category:** Enemy Units & Architecture
    
      
    
- **Lore Details:** Unlike military warships designed to incinerate targets, the Harvesting Fleet is engineered for **zero-bruise livestock preservation**.
    
      
    - **Collector Drones:** Flying units that fire concentrated bio-electric stun orbs—visually similar to the harmless energy balls the Niiikos play with, but calibrated to instantly paralyze a 1.2m nervous system without damaging valuable organs.
        
          
        
    - **Vacuum Harvesters:** Heavy quadrupedal spider-machines that deploy electrified restraint cables and suction tubes to load unconscious Niiikos into orbital transport pods.
        
          
        
    - **The Orbital AA Battery:** A massive, automated kinetic-laser turret dropped at the planet's high ridge. Its sole protocol is airspace denial—programmed to automatically obliterate any unauthorized vessel attempting to breach the upper cloud ceiling.
        
          
        

### The Mad Dog Gang (Outer Rim Syndicate)

- **Category:** Criminal Underworld
    
      
    
- **Lore Details:** The ruthless smuggling and poaching cartel Ethan worked for twenty years ago before his fateful crash. Operating out of lawless asteroid belts, the Mad Dogs specialize in forged colonial identity chips, black-market organ runners, and illegal slave auctions. Throughout the first half of the game, Ethan’s goal is to repair his sub-space radio to send the planet's coordinates to Vance, the Mad Dog boss—a call that would have bought Ethan a clean citizen record at the cost of putting every Niiiko in butcher cages.
    
      
    

### Orbital Penal Station "Tartarus-9" & The Subhuman Registry

- **Category:** Colonial Law & Background
    
      
    
- **Lore Details:** In the human colonies, serious felons are stripped of their legal species status. Under the _Penal Reclamation Act_, convicts have their citizen ID chips burned out and replaced with a collar barcode. They are legally classified as "Subhuman Industrial Assets." Fed gray recycled protein sludge and kept in zero-gravity dark tanks when off-shift, inmates have no legal right to property, speech, or self-defense. This institutional dehumanization is the root of Ethan’s twenty-year psychological obsession with obtaining a valid plastic ID card.
    
      
    

### Sanctuary Sector-04 & The Plastic Name Tags

- **Category:** Environment & Cultural Lore
    
      
    
- **Lore Details:** Fifty years prior to Ethan's crash, a corporate bio-research team abandoned Sector-04 after a budget restructuring, leaving behind the first generation of lab-grown Niiikos. Every Niiiko wears a durable, color-coded plastic tag clipped to their tunic or collar. While Ethan immediately recognizes them as laboratory specimen barcodes used to track livestock lineage, the innocent Niiikos evolved to view the tags as sacred gifts of identity—proof that each of them has a unique name and a place in the village family.
    
      
    

### The Arsenal Progression

- **Category:** Gameplay Mechanics & Weapons
    
      
    
- **Lore Details:**
    
      
    1. **Concentrated Energy Balls:** Naturally occurring bio-luminescent plasma spheres formed by local flora and Niiiko enchanters. Soft and bouncy when thrown by hand, used by villagers for play and light tasks, capable of temporarily short-circuiting weak scout optics.
        
          
        
    2. **Tania's Pneumatic Launcher:** A mid-game improvised weapon built from hydraulic ship pipes. It compresses energy balls into high-velocity bolts capable of knocking back collector drones, though it suffers from slow pressure recharge.
        
          
        
    3. **The Scrap-Iron Gunpowder Rifle ("The Bone-Breaker"):** Tania’s late-game masterpiece. Combining sulfur, charcoal, and potassium nitrate refined from the planet's caverns, this crude, heavy-caliber bolt-action rifle fires solid lead-iron slugs. Because it lacks modern colonial recoil-dampeners, its kickback would shatter a Niiiko's spine; only Ethan's heavy human frame can shoulder it, tearing straight through corporate drone plating at the cost of bruising his own flesh with every shot.
        
          
        

### The Rogue Shuttle ("Iron Hound")

- **Category:** Vehicle & Franchise Bridge
    
      
    
- **Lore Details:** A cramped, short-range colonial prison transport stolen by Ethan during the Tartarus-9 riot. Built for a two-man human guard crew, its controls are heavy, analog, and elevated far above a 1.2-meter Niiiko's reach. When Ethan locks Tania and Lily inside during the climax and engages the blind emergency hyper-jump, the jury-rigged drive tears through dimensional space—causing the shuttle to crash-land directly into the foggy backyard garden of Mai's world in _Forever Alone_, with several vital manifold screws shaken loose on impact.
