Plot: Mai is a normal high school girl, but her parents mistook her and hurt her emotional a lot. One night they fight and she said she hate her parents and wish everyone disappear. And that happen, she alone wake up and see everything is fog. She try to move, understand world, cause of it, and how to redeem.

Art style:
Chibi, 3D, Horror


Selling point:
When a player scrolls past hundreds of indie titles on Steam, your store page needs to answer one question immediately: _Why this game instead of ten other horror titles?_

- **The Mechanical Reality Swap when close eyes try to sleep**
    
    She cant do it at first as. the horror will only happen if she meet the event . And then she can swap reality by try to sleep.
    
- **The Visual Contrast: Chibi Fragility vs. Rotting Guilt.**
    
    A cute, stylized diorama character walking through soft, quiet fog who suddenly triggers a grotesque, shifting material rot creates instant visual shock. That contrast makes high-retention GIFs and TikTok clips that stop people from scrolling.
    
- **The Emotional Stakes: The Granted Wish.**
    
    Most horror games use external monsters (cults, demons, serial killers). Your narrative hook is guilt: _A teenage girl wished her parents would disappear, woke up alone in the fog, and now has to explore their private struggles to redeem what she destroyed._
